What's on

Talks, a tournament, and a floor that never closes early

Panels run 11:00–17:00 in the old grain silo. From 21:00 the arcade takes over two rooms on Schiekade and runs until 02:00.

  • 01

    Talks in the silo

    Eleven talks across two days, 25 minutes each, no slides longer than nine words. All recorded and posted free within a week.

  • 02

    The 64-player tournament

    One game, single elimination, Saturday from 13:00. Bring your own stick or use ours. Sign-up closes when the bracket fills, usually by Friday evening.

  • 03

    Late arcade

    Two rooms on Schiekade, 21:00–02:00 Friday and Saturday. Thirty-one cabinets and a wall of CRTs, all on free play with your wristband.

  • 04

    Quiet room

    A dimmed room on the second floor with beanbags, water, and no sound above 45 decibels. Open whenever the building is. No booking, no queue.

  • 05

    Build clinic

    Sunday 10:00–13:00: bring a project, sit with two working developers for 40 minutes, leave with a list. Free, twelve slots, sign up at the door.
