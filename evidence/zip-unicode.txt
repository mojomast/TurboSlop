## Conclusion
- The ARCHIVE is valid: three independent readers (Info-ZIP in two locales, Python zipfile,
  busybox) verify CRCs and extract Unicode names byte-correct. Not an archive defect.
- The "?" characters busybox prints are console-encoding display behaviour, not corruption.
- The one reproducible hard failure is ERR_INVALID_CHAR for a non-latin1 download filename in
  an HTTP header — impossible for our ASCII slugs and encoded by contentDisposition() regardless.
